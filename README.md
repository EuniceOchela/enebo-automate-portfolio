## Case Study 1: Client Onboarding & Follow-Up Automation

**The Problem:**
A business collecting leads through a form had no automated way to move new contacts into their CRM, track deal progress, or follow up once a deal closed, everything relied on manual entry and memory.

**The Solution:**
Two connected n8n workflows that handle the full journey from lead capture to onboarding:

1. **Lead Capture Workflow**
   - Trigger: Tally form webhook
   - Action: Automatically creates/updates a contact in HubSpot and creates a new deal — no manual data entry required

2. **Onboarding Workflow**
   - Trigger: Scheduled check
   - Action: Pulls deals from HubSpot, filters by deal stage, retrieves the associated contact, and sends a personalized welcome email via Gmail once a deal closes

**Tools used:** n8n, HubSpot CRM, Tally Forms, Gmail

**Outcome:** New leads are captured and organized automatically, and no closed deal falls through the cracks without a timely welcome — freeing up hours that would otherwise go to manual follow-up.
